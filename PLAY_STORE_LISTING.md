## Full description

FocusHaven is a calm, supportive space for doing one thing at a time.

Choose a Focus, Short Break, or Long Break session, set a custom duration, and let a gentle timer guide your rhythm. Whether you are studying, working, creating, or simply finding a quieter moment, FocusHaven helps you return to what matters without pressure.

Focus with intention

- Set an intention before a session begins.
- Choose focus, short-break, and long-break timers.
- Set custom minutes and seconds for the time you actually have.
- Pause, reset, and resume sessions with confidence.

Make space for distractions

- Park distracting thoughts without losing them.
- Return to your saved thoughts after a session.
- Build a simple focus queue and complete tasks at your own pace.

Notice your progress

- See recent sessions, daily focus time, streaks, and milestones.
- Set a daily goal that feels realistic for you.
- Review your focus history and completed tasks.

Reflect gently

- Capture a short journal reflection and your mood.
- Use guided breathing when you need a moment to reset.
- Receive gentle session-complete notifications when enabled.

Make it yours

- Choose from calming appearance themes.
- Use FocusHaven as a guest, or sign in with Google for optional cloud backup and restore.
- Optional FocusHaven Pro features may be available through in-app purchase.

FocusHaven is designed to be encouraging, flexible, and human. No guilt—just a calm place to begin again.
