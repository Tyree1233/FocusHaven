## Product voice

FocusHaven should sound calm, respectful, concise, non-punitive, and honest
about uncertainty. It must not shame a person, diagnose a condition, promise an
outcome, invent completed work, or imply that an optional suggestion is a
command.

The reviewer should prefer natural Spanish over word-for-word English while
preserving every safety, privacy, consent, confirmation, and agency boundary.
The intended initial audience is general international Spanish; country-specific
wording must be documented rather than silently mixed.

## High-risk language review

The reviewer must explicitly sign off on these categories rather than relying
on general fluency review:

- delete account, cloud backup, local data, and irreversible-action warnings;
- purchase, restore, entitlement, price, and unavailable-product messages;
- microphone, speech-recognition, notification, calendar, and Focus Shield
  permission boundaries;
- exact confirmation and replay-protection language for Haven actions;
- safety and care responses that direct a person away from coaching when
  appropriate;
- privacy statements for journals, reflections, transcripts, account identity,
  calendar availability, and Enhanced AI;
- uncertain or advisory language for Rhythm, Forecast, Planner, Journey,
  Window, Shield, and Smart Reset; and
- accessibility labels whose wording changes what a control appears to do.

## Grammar and formatting review

The full review must cover:

- singular, plural, and zero forms for sessions, tasks, minutes, seconds,
  milestones, moods, and history;
- placeholder order and agreement around names, counts, dates, times, prices,
  task titles, and durations;
- 12-hour and 24-hour time presentation without changing stored values;
- locale-aware dates, weekdays, decimal conventions, and punctuation;
- neutral or inclusive phrasing where the English source does not identify
  gender; and
- compact controls, narrow phones, large accessibility text, and screen-reader
  output.

## Private-data exclusion

The review package may contain only the source catalog, approved context, and
synthetic examples. It must not include real tasks, journal entries,
reflections, transcripts, coaching conversations, account identities, calendar
events, purchases, or focus history.
